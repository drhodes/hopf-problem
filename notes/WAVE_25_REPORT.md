1. **Algebraic Dimension and Néron-Severi Group (Theorem 9.1(i), Proposition 9.7)**:

   - The very general fibre $F_z$ has Néron-Severi group $\mathrm{NS}(F_z) \cong \mathbb{Z}\eta$, with signature $(1, 1)$, ruling out positive line bundles and giving fiber algebraic dimension $a(F_z) = 0$.

   - Canonical line bundle $K_X \cong f^* \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1}(-1) \otimes \mathcal{O}_X(2S_2)$.
   - Non-torsion proof: $K_X^{\otimes 4k} \cong f^* \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1}(-2k)$ has negative degree for all $k \ge 1$, hence $H^0(X, K_X^{\otimes 4k}) = 0$.

   - $R^2 f_* \mathcal{O}_X \cong \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1}(-1)$
